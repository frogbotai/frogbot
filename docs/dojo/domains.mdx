Every app has an address under `frogbot.app`, such as `deals.frogbot.app`. An app's Admin can also serve it at a domain they own.

## Add a domain

1. In the app's settings, enter a subdomain such as `crm.example.com`, or run `npx frogbot dojo domains set deals crm.example.com`.
2. At your DNS provider, add the CNAME record Dojo shows: `crm.example.com` pointing at `deals.frogbot.app`.
3. Wait. Dojo checks the record, gets a certificate, and serves the app there.

The status moves from **Waiting for DNS** to **Getting certificate** to **Live**. If the record later stops pointing at the app, the domain shows **Offline** until you fix it. `npx frogbot dojo domains get deals` shows the record to add and the current status.

## Rules

- Use a subdomain with at least three parts, such as `crm.example.com`. Root domains such as `example.com`, wildcards and IP addresses aren't supported.
- Domains under `frogbot.app` and `frogbot.ai` are Dojo's own.
- An app has one custom domain. Setting another replaces it, after asking.
- A [free preview](/dojo/deploy#free-previews-and-paid-apps) has no custom domain. Keep it as a paid app, then add one.

To remove the domain, clear it on the same page or run `npx frogbot dojo domains clear deals`. The app keeps its `frogbot.app` address throughout.
