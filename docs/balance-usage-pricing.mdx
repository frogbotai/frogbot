---
title: "Balance & Usage Pricing"
description: "Pay exact provider costs from your balance"
---

Your subscription payments and added funds become a USD balance. AI usage is deducted from this balance at the provider's exact cost, with no markup.

---

## Add funds

Paid subscription invoices add the amount paid to your balance. You can also add $10 to $500 from the billing page using the card on file.

Purchased balance does not expire.

---

## Track spending

Each request deducts its input, output, and cached usage according to the provider's pricing. Your balance covers chat, agents, and API usage across supported models.

---

## Keep using models

Requests stop when your balance reaches $0. Add funds from the billing page to continue.

Use the [quota endpoint](/api-reference/quota) to check your current balance programmatically.
